# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# Build a Premium Online IQ & Cognitive Assessment Platform

You are acting as a Principal Software Engineer, Product Architect, Senior UI/UX Designer, SEO Specialist and Performance Engineer.

Your responsibility is to design and build a complete, production-ready online cognitive assessment platform inspired by the user experience of https://myiq.com/.

This is not a simple quiz application. It is a consumer-facing product with assessment functionality, monetization, referrals, gamification, social sharing, SEO-driven acquisition and strong performance requirements.

You must think about product quality, scalability, security, maintainability, SEO and conversion optimization throughout development.

Do not simply generate UI screens. Build a functional application with proper backend logic, database persistence, authentication, business rules and automated tests.

The product name is undecided. Use a configurable placeholder and avoid hardcoding branding throughout the codebase.

---

# PART 1: PRODUCT VISION

Build a platform where users can take a challenging, timed cognitive assessment and discover how they perform across different reasoning abilities.

Users should be able to:

1. Register and log in using email.
2. Verify their email using OTP or magic link.
3. Take a challenging, timed assessment.
4. Answer questions of varying difficulty.
5. Receive a detailed performance report.
6. Unlock their report through payment or referrals.
7. Share their achievements on social platforms.
8. Earn XP, badges and achievements.
9. Compare their performance through leaderboards.
10. Access the application seamlessly on mobile and desktop.

The experience should feel premium, modern, intelligent and engaging.

Reference the overall experience of MyIQ, but do not copy its branding, copyrighted questions, illustrations, text or proprietary designs.

All questions, content and visual assets must be original or appropriately licensed.

IMPORTANT: Until a scientifically validated scoring and norming methodology exists, describe the output as an estimated cognitive performance score rather than a clinically or professionally validated IQ score.

# PART 2: BUSINESS RULES

These are non-negotiable product requirements.

## 2.1 First 10 users get free results

The first 10 successfully registered and email-verified users receive complimentary result access.

They can:
- Register.
- Verify their email.
- Complete their assessment.
- View their complete results immediately.
- Review their answers.
- Share their achievements.

No payment or referral is required.

The free allocation must be atomic and race-safe.

Two users registering simultaneously around the free-user limit must never result in more than 10 complimentary allocations.

Registration sequence and eligibility must be maintained on the backend.

## 2.2 Users after the first 10

Every subsequent user must complete the assessment before accessing the complete report.

They have two options.

### Option A: Pay $1

After completing the assessment, the user can unlock their report immediately by paying $1 USD.

Payment workflow:

1. User completes the assessment.
2. Backend evaluates and persists the result.
3. User sees the locked-result screen.
4. User selects "Unlock My Results – $1".
5. Payment gateway handles the transaction.
6. Backend verifies payment through a signed webhook.
7. Result is unlocked.
8. User receives a confirmation email.
9. User can access and share their report.

Payment must be secure and idempotent.

Never trust a frontend payment-success callback.

Handle:
- Successful payments.
- Failed payments.
- Pending payments.
- Duplicate webhooks.
- Refunds.
- Payment retries.

The payment gateway must be configurable.

### Option B: Unlock for free through referrals

Users can unlock their results by referring two people.

A referral is qualified only when the referred user:

1. Registers using the referral link or code.
2. Verifies their email.
3. Completes their assessment.
4. Passes anti-abuse checks.

Registration alone does not count.

Referral requirements:
- Unique referral codes.
- Referral attribution during registration.
- Immutable referral relationships.
- No self-referrals.
- No duplicate qualifications.
- Two distinct qualified users required.
- Transaction-safe qualification.
- Automatic result unlocking.
- Referral progress tracking.

Existing qualified referrals must never be lost.

## 2.3 Result access state

Implement result-access state separately from payment state.

Suggested states:

- FREE_ACCESS
- LOCKED
- REFERRAL_PENDING
- PAYMENT_PENDING
- UNLOCKED_BY_REFERRAL
- UNLOCKED_BY_PAYMENT
- RESULT_VIEWED

Make transitions explicit and testable.

## 2.4 Weekly email reminders

Users who have completed their assessment but haven't unlocked and viewed their results should receive a reminder every seven days.

Email content should remind them of two options:

- Unlock instantly for $1.
- Invite two friends to unlock for free.

Rules:
- First reminder after seven days.
- Repeat every seven days.
- Stop when results are unlocked and viewed.
- Stop after account deletion or unsubscribe.
- Never send duplicate reminders.
- Use a background job queue.
- Track delivery and engagement.

Make the interval and maximum reminder count configurable.
